Huezel Support

Huezel is a color block puzzle game. Need help? We're happy to hear from you.

Contact: support@kelbrasolutions.net. Please include your phone model and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

Frequently asked questions

My progress disappeared. Can I get it back?

Your levels, stars and boosters are saved on your device. On the main menu, tap Save / restore progress to copy a code you can paste back in later, or on a new phone. If you play in a web browser, saved data can be erased when browser data is cleared, so installing the app or adding Huezel to your Home Screen is recommended.

How do I get 3 stars?

Finish a level using few pieces. The level card and the badge at the top right show how many pieces you need.

What is a Pure clear?

Fill a whole row or column with blocks of ONE color. It scores triple and blows up touching blocks of that color.

How do I get boosters, and why are some locked?

Boosters unlock one at a time as you climb the Journey (Rewind at level 3, Block Breaker 5, Color Changer 7, Shuffle 9, Rainbow Wave 11, Bomb 13), and each one starts with a short hands-on lesson. You earn them by playing: clearing levels (more on Hard), getting three stars, finishing a world and solving the Weekly Challenge. You can also buy booster packs in the Shop.

Why can't I open a world?

The six Journey worlds open through the year to match the seasons: Sunny Meadow is open now, Frozen Peaks opens December 14, then Candy Kingdom, Coral Reef, Volcano Rush and Harvest Hollow. Each world's menu card shows when it opens.

I bought something and did not get it

Open Shop → Store → Restore purchases. This re-checks your App Store or Google Play account and gives you back anything you bought. If it still does not appear, email us with your order receipt from Apple or Google.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (play.google.com, Order history). We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but we are happy to help if you are stuck.

The leaderboard shows a name that is not okay

Tap the Report button next to the name. We review reports and replace names that break our rules.

How do I change or remove my leaderboard name?

Change the name in the main menu. To have your leaderboard data deleted, email us with the game ID shown under Save / restore progress in the main menu.

Does Huezel need an internet connection?

No. After the first install the game works offline. Only leaderboards, online backup and purchases need a connection.
