Huezel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Huezel is a color block puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game uses.

What stays on your device

Huezel does not ask you to create an account, and it never asks for your email, location, contacts, photos, or your phone's advertising ID.

The game saves your progress (levels, stars, boosters, sparks, streaks, trophies, settings, and the optional name you type) on your own device, in the browser or app storage. Clearing the app's data or site data deletes it.

Optional online features

Some features use the internet and are optional. They are used only when they are turned on in your version of the game.

  • Leaderboards. When you finish a ranked run (Daily Time Trial, Daily Dash, Weekly Challenge or Classic), the game sends our server a random ID it made for your device (not your phone's hardware ID), the display name you chose, and the list of moves you made, so the server can check your score is real. The display name and score are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Names that break our rules are replaced. You can play without ever opening the leaderboards, and you can change or remove your name in the menu.
  • Online backup. If you turn it on, your saved progress is stored online under a random code, so you can restore it on another phone. It contains no name or email.
  • Challenge links. If you tap "Challenge a friend", the link you share contains your score, your name (if you entered one) and a game seed. It goes only where you send it.

This data is stored with our hosting providers (Cloudflare and Google Firebase), is encrypted while it travels, is not sold, and is not shared with advertisers. We keep leaderboard data while the game is available. To delete your data, email us from the address below with the game ID shown on the "Save / restore progress" screen, or the display name you used,, and we will remove it.

Sharing

If you tap Share Result, the game creates a text message with your score (and your optional name). It is sent only where you choose to send it.

Ads and purchases

Huezel has no ads. The game is free to play, and the app versions offer optional in-app purchases (spark packs, booster packs, a starter pack and cosmetic skins). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your card or payment details. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was made, so we can give you what you bought, and we keep a record of what you own on your device. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated first to list the ad provider and what it collects.

Children

Huezel is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13.
